# Roadmap & Milestones

## Phase 1 -- Earth Core (Months 1-3)

### Month 1: Foundation
- Static handcrafted continent map (30-40 regions initially, expand to 100-120)
- 3 civilizations with starting positions
- 1 year per turn simulation engine
- Population growth formula
- Basic stability system

### Month 2: Conflict & Expansion
- Expansion logic (AI and player)
- Auto-resolve war system
- Supply simplified to adjacency to capital
- Terrain-based defense modifiers

### Month 3: Heroes & Polish
- Hero system (3 types: General, Reformer, Visionary)
- Golden age mechanic
- Fast-forward (5x / 10x)
- 500-year stability benchmark
- Save/load system

### Definition of Done
- 500-year stable simulation without crash
- At least one collapse event across 10 runs
- At least one golden age observed across 10 runs
- 10-year fast-forward < 2 seconds

## Phase 2 -- Logistics & Resource Depth (Months 4-6)

- Supply tracing from capital through regions
- Terrain-based attrition
- Resource depletion (coal, oil, rare earths)
- Infrastructure impact on production
- Renewable resource degradation with overuse

## Phase 3 -- Climate & Industrial Pressure (Future)

- Environmental stress systems
- Migration patterns
- Industrial tradeoffs
- Advanced ideology branches

## Phase 4 -- Solar Expansion (Future)

- Orbital regions
- Planetary colonies
- Alien civilizations
- Tactical battle layer (optional engagement)
