# Hidden Tech Emergence (Aligned)

## Philosophy
Technology is emergent. Players do not select techs; they emerge when systemic thresholds are met.

## Hidden Metrics (0–100)
- Knowledge
- Energy
- Social Coordination
- Economic Surplus
- Military Pressure

## Emergence Process
1) Check thresholds per tech
2) Compute pressure multiplier (overshoot)
3) Random roll → tech may emerge

## Notes
- Metrics grow from population, stability, production, war pressure
- Multiple techs can emerge in the same year
- Tech effects are currently minimal or implicit (future hooks)
