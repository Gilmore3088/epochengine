# Heroes & Golden Ages (Aligned)

## Hero Types

| Type | Modifier | Effect |
|------|----------|--------|
| General | +10% military | Boosts combat strength |
| Reformer | +5 stability | Adds directly to stability |
| Visionary | +10% production | Boosts production output |

## Hero Lifecycle
- Spawn chance scales with stability and population
- Age increments yearly
- Lifespan randomized (40–80)
- Max 3 active heroes per civ

## Golden Age
**Trigger Conditions:**
- Stability > 80
- Food and production stockpiles positive
- No active wars

**Effects:**
- +50% food
- +50% production
- Stability floor applied

**End Conditions:**
- Stability drops below threshold or war declared

**Cooldown:** 30 years
