# Game Design Document

## Core Loop (Per Year)

1. Population Growth
2. Resource Production
3. Stability Update
4. Diplomacy Update
5. War Resolution
6. Hero Aging
7. Tech Emergence Check

## World Types

- **Historical Earth** -- Handcrafted regions based on real geography
- **Procedural Earth-like** -- (Future phase)
- **Custom** -- Pangea, Archipelago, Mountain-heavy, Water World (Future phase)

## Victory / End States

- **Planetary Unification** -- One civilization controls all regions
- **Technological Transcendence** -- Civilization reaches highest tech tier
- **Galactic Dominance** -- (Future phase) Space expansion victory
- **Total Collapse** -- All civilizations fall (game over)

## Player Actions (V0.1)

- Declare War
- Offer Alliance
- Adjust Military Investment
- Advance Year
- Toggle Fast Forward (5x / 10x)

## Player Role

The player observes and nudges a civilization through high-level decisions. The simulation drives most outcomes emergently. The player does NOT micromanage units, build queues, or select technologies.
